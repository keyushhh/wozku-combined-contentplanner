# Wozku Knowledge Base

Mirror of the public knowledge base at `https://wozku.com/kb`, captured 7 August 2026. Fifty-six articles across ten sections, in `reference/`.

## The single most important thing to understand

**This KB describes the LIVE production Wozku platform. The repository you are working in is a prototype being built toward it.**

The two are not in conflict. The KB is the **target state**. Where the prototype lacks something the KB describes, that is a feature not yet built, not a bug and not a contradiction. Where the prototype does something differently, the KB is the reference for what it should eventually become.

Consequences for any task:

1. **Never "fix" the prototype to remove something because the KB does not mention it, or assume a KB feature already exists in the repo.** Check `reference/BUILD-BACKLOG.md` first. It maps every KB capability to actual repo state.
2. **Use KB vocabulary as canonical.** When the repo and the KB name the same thing differently, the KB wins for product conversations and the repo wins for code. Both mappings are in the backlog file.
3. **The KB is buyer-facing and admin-facing.** It documents behaviour, not implementation. It deliberately does not disclose the Wozku Score formula. Do not invent one.

## What Wozku is, per the KB

Wozku is the operating platform for **Advocacy-Led Growth (ALG)**: the category of B2B marketing where reach is generated by a brand's own people, employees, customers, partners and event attendees, sharing content to their own networks, in their own voice, from their own authenticated accounts.

The KB defines the category as an umbrella:

> Advocacy-Led Growth = Employee Advocacy + Customer Advocacy + Partner Advocacy + Community Advocacy

What ALG adds over those four pre-existing motions is a unified operating model: one platform, one authenticated-share mechanic, one Wozku Score, one leaderboard, regardless of whether today's advocate is an employee, a customer, or a partner.

### What the platform actually does

| Capability | Mechanic |
| --- | --- |
| Publishes your copy through other people's accounts | Participant scans a QR or clicks a link, authorises the channel once via OAuth, the post publishes natively on their feed |
| Scores every share | A **Wozku Score** from a proprietary model, applied uniformly across all channels |
| Projects a live leaderboard | Contest screen cast to a monitor, projector or kiosk, with podium, join QR, and live share feed |
| Captures leads | Gate the share behind a form, or run **LeadSpark** where the form is the front door |
| Reports dollar value | ROI Calculator converts reach, clicks and form fills into **Earned Media Value** |

### Why people participate

Four levers, and every gamification feature maps to one:

- **Visible standing.** A projected leaderboard makes effort public.
- **Stakes.** A podium, prize or recognition makes position matter.
- **Speed.** Under 60 seconds from QR scan to published post.
- **Fair recognition.** The Wozku Score caps audience size, so a 300-follower advocate with real engagement can out-rank a 10,000-follower account that posts and walks away.

### What Wozku is explicitly not

The KB is unusually precise here and it matters for scoping. Wozku is **not** an employee-advocacy scheduling tool (no content library, no queue, no calendar), **not** a social scheduler, **not** a company-page manager (never posts as a brand), **not** an ad platform, **not** an influencer marketplace, **not** a referral tool (1-to-many public sharing, not 1-to-1 nudges), **not** an event platform, and **not** a CRM. Full reasoning in `reference/01-getting-started.md`.

## Core vocabulary

Use these exact terms and this exact capitalisation.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Contest** | Live-event mode. Four-step wizard. Has a public screen, leaderboard, Go Live / Pause / Stop, and the Additional Settings layer (Coupons, Forms, Quiz, Booths). |
| **Campaign** | Always-on mode. Three-step wizard. No screen, no leaderboard, no lifecycle controls. Adds a Campaign-exclusive **Header Image** at 1920x400px. |
| **LeadSpark** | A standalone global form with the Contest Form toggle on. Lead capture is the front door, sharing is the reward mechanic. |
| **Wozku Score** | Per-share score, aggregated to a per-campaign total and a global lifetime total. Proprietary AI model, 13+ parameters. **Weights and inputs are not publicly disclosed. Do not invent a formula.** |
| **Potential Reach** | Sum of first-degree audience across every participant who has shared, summed per channel, captured at share-authorisation time. No second-degree multiplier. |
| **Earned Media Value** | (Potential Reach x CPM / 1000) + (Clicks x CPC) + (Form fills x CPL). |
| **Credit** | The billing meter. One credit is consumed per completed authenticated share. |
| **Channel Ninja URL** | `wozku.com/share/{channel}/{hash}`. Routes straight to one channel's OAuth consent screen with no Wozku landing page in between. |
| **Universal Share URL** | `wozku.com/share/post/{hash}`. Lightweight channel picker. |
| **Public URL** | `wozku.com/post/{hash}`. Full landing page with social proof grid and post preview. |
| **Booster Post** | A post configured to award extra points. |
| **Hold & Fire** | Stage all posts, release simultaneously at a specified UTC datetime. |
| **UGC mode** | Participants write their own version of the post instead of sharing preset copy. |
| **Winner Finalization** | The workflow triggered by Stop on a Contest. Default or custom criteria. |

## The three product modes

**Contest** when the event is live and physical. Carries the full stack.

**Campaign** when the programme is always-on or email-driven. Everything a Contest has except the screen layer, the lifecycle controls, Theme Settings, Embed Code, Winner Finalization, and the Additional Settings tabs.

**LeadSpark** when lead capture must come first and the share second.

**Critical modelling note.** The KB presents Contest and Campaign as two types chosen at creation and **not convertible afterward**. But both live at `/admin/campaigns/{ID}` and both use `campaigns/{hash}` URLs, and the KB states "a Contest starts from a campaign record." So underneath they are **one record type with an immutable mode discriminator**, not two entities. Model them that way. The repo's single `Campaign` entity is correct in shape; what it lacks is the discriminator.

## The five channels

| Channel | Status | Default B2B use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LinkedIn** | GA | The default channel for B2B. Professional advocacy, event amplification, thought leadership. |
| **Twitter / X** | GA | Real-time event broadcasting, live hashtags, developer relations. |
| **Slack** | Beta | Internal advocacy. SKOs, advisory boards, internal launches. |
| **Facebook** | Private Beta, gated | Community-driven verticals, regional B2B, Group activation. |
| **Instagram** | Private Beta, gated | Visual-first moments. Requires a Business or Creator account. |

A single Contest or Campaign can enable one channel, any combination, or all five. Channel selection is **per post**.

## URL reference

Every pattern the platform generates. Reproduce these exactly.

| Purpose | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Public URL, per post | `https://wozku.com/post/{hash}` |
| Universal Share URL, per post | `https://wozku.com/share/post/{hash}` |
| Channel Ninja URL, per post per channel | `https://wozku.com/share/{channel}/{hash}` |
| Campaign public landing page | `https://wozku.com/campaigns/{hash}` |
| Contest screen | `https://wozku.com/campaigns/screen/{hash}` |
| Single-post screen | `https://wozku.com/post/screen/{post-hash}` |
| Public dashboard | `https://wozku.com/dashboard/campaign/{ID}` |
| Admin | `https://wozku.com/admin` |

The `screen/` segment is what distinguishes a Contest from a Campaign in a public URL.

## Known gaps in the source KB

Flag these rather than filling them in.

- **The Facebook and Instagram channel articles are truncated upstream.** They publish a heading and intro, then jump to Next Step. The promised body (OAuth scopes, post types, character and media limits, engagement signals) does not exist in the page HTML. What is known about those two channels was reconstructed from the Overview and Ninja URL articles and is marked as such.
- **Ninja URL channel status labels are inconsistent between articles.** `sharing-urls` lists Facebook and Instagram as Beta; `links-modal` and `qr-codes` list them as Private Beta. Both are preserved as written. Private Beta appears correct, since it matches the Overview and the What is Wozku article.
- **The KB never states literal OAuth scope strings** for any channel. It describes permissions in prose only, including explicit non-grants (no DMs, no message history, no follow-relationship modification, no LinkedIn Messaging). Do not fabricate scope names.
- **Rate limits are almost entirely non-numeric.** The only concrete figure is Zoom returning HTTP 429 on registrations.
- **The Wozku Score formula is deliberately undisclosed.** 13+ parameters, AI engine, audience cap. That is the full extent of what is public.
